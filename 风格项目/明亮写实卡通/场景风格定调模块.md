# 拟人动物城市｜场景风格定调模块

状态：**通用视觉定调已获用户方向认可；本文件是从既有风格规范与剧目 ABC v03 提炼的可复用词库，不等于某张场景图已经出图获认可。** 默认写实仿真人任务不加载本文件。这里说的“动物城”是用户沟通偏好的简称；正式英文提示词只写原创可见设计，不出现参照作品、工作室、角色或标志性建筑。

## 一句话核心锚句

> An original cinematic stylized 3D animated animal-city environment with gently softened, expressive sculpted architectural silhouettes, visibly designed yet usable proportions, broad coordinated color planes, softly painted tactile materials, readable functional staging enriched with curated signs of everyday use, clearly distinguishable action landmarks, luminous color bounce, and graphic shadows grounded in believable light sources.

这句是**风格骨架，不是完整场景提示词**。单独复制它无法确定空间功能、机位、动线、剧情情绪或具体色板。

## 模块拆解

| 模块 | 负责什么 | 可直接嵌入英文提示词的句子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ① 画面媒介 | 指定原创动画画面，而非实景效果图 | `An original cinematic stylized 3D animated environment with polished CGI depth and clear feature-animation art direction.` |
| ② 建筑形状 | 缩略图可见的动画感 | `Build the space from expressive sculpted silhouettes, layered volumes, slightly enlarged portals and a deliberate rhythm of curves and angled planes; keep the construction and circulation believable.` |
| ③ 色彩组织 | 饱和但不花哨 | `Organize the scene into broad, coordinated color planes with sophisticated saturation: one dominant color family, a supporting contrast, and a restrained accent at the focal point or route of movement.` |
| ④ 材质与细节 | 保留触感，防止楼盘写实化 | `Render stone, plaster, wood, ceramic and metal as softened matte or satin animated surfaces with selective tactile texture; let form and color read before seams, grain and reflections.` |
| ⑤ 有源光影 | 保留原项目的光影体系 | `Let [visible source] illuminate [specific surfaces] from [direction]; use [sky or practical fill] to keep the space readable, colored bounce to separate planes, and soft graphic shadows with convincing contact.` |
| ⑥ 物种尺度 | 只在功能相关处使用 | `Size doors, seating, controls and turning space for the named upright animal residents where their body differences matter; keep fixtures practical rather than decorative animal motifs.` |
| ⑦ 情绪覆盖 | 根据剧本改变明暗、色温和秩序 | `Express [story mood] through [architectural hierarchy, color balance and light placement], while keeping the action area and key objects clearly readable.` |
| ⑧ 动作参照物 | 座位、门、操作区能分别指认 | `Place exactly [count] action-related [objects] at [separate visible positions], distinguished by [specific color, silhouette or material]; preserve clear gaps, usable access and stable spatial relationships.` |
| ⑨ 构图与中等细节 | 表演重点清楚，场所保留美感与生活层次 | `Give [core functional object and action area] a clearly readable scale and silhouette in the frame; retain convincing functional furnishings, group signs of everyday use and selective ornament in secondary areas, control their visual contrast, and preserve the venue's believable capacity.` |
| ⑩ 克制亲和感 | 轻微增加卡通可爱，服从场所情绪 | `Use gently appealing contours, selective proportion exaggeration and a little rhythmic asymmetry, with softly painted tactile surfaces and coordinated saturated color planes; keep the space usable and appropriate to [story mood].` |

### 两种常用的情绪覆盖

- **明快、舒适的都市空间**：`Use luminous daylight, confident but controlled warm–cool color contrast, playful architectural rhythm and gentle color bounce. The city feels inviting and alive while remaining spatially believable.`
- **冷清或压迫的剧情空间**：`Keep the palette cool and the composition more controlled; create tension through enclosing geometry, repeated structural rhythm and concentrated warm accents. Maintain readable midtones and clear source lighting instead of turning the image dark or desaturated.`

“黑手党豪宅”只属于具体剧目的额外覆盖：可用厚重门洞、轴线、对称或围合的结构秩序、深色家具与有限金属饰面体现权势；**压迫感由形状与空间关系承担，不靠欠曝**。不要把这个覆盖套到学校、公寓或普通街道。

## 拼装顺序

项目硬限制继续生效：不出现现实国家国旗、国徽及可识别官方国家徽记。 该限制针对真实国家标识，不泛化为禁用所有旗帜或原创徽章；经核验且有画面用途的原创虚构机构／组织标识，仍按现有规则使用。 正式单条提示词写明：`No real-world national flags, coats of arms, or recognizable official national emblems.`。

`固定 16:9 与实际场景身份 → 空间功能、核心动作物与机位 → ⑨中等细节构图及⑧参照物 → ②建筑形状与⑩克制亲和感 → ③色板 → ④材质 → ⑤光源 → ⑥必要尺度 → ⑦剧情情绪 → 空镜与项目硬限制`

英文提示词正文第一句直接写：`A fixed 16:9 landscape frame composed across its full horizontal width.` 接着填当前场景的地点、功能和独有结构。颜色尽量用 **4—8 个与真实材质绑定的 Hex 色值**，不要把某栋公寓的奶油、珊瑚、青绿配色复制为所有剧目的统一色板。

### 可复制的组装模板

```text
A fixed 16:9 landscape frame composed across its full horizontal width. An empty [specific functional space and story location] in an original cinematic stylized 3D animated animal city. [Camera position and direction, giving the core functional object and action area a clearly readable scale.] Retain [convincing functional furnishings and grouped signs of everyday use in secondary areas], with [exact count, separate visible positions and distinct colors or silhouettes of action-related landmarks]. Build the scene around [one distinctive sculpted architectural silhouette] and [two or three readable foreground, midground and background shapes], with [clear access, standing and circulation areas]. Use gently appealing contours and selective proportion exaggeration with believable construction, broad coordinated color planes, softly painted matte or satin materials and selective tactile detail. [Name 4–8 colors, each tied to a specific material or object.] [Visible light source] enters from [direction] and falls across [surfaces]; [sky or practical fill] keeps [important areas] readable, while colored bounce and soft graphic shadows preserve convincing depth and contact. Keep secondary objects lower in contrast so [core functional object] remains the focal point. [Only if needed: explain how the named species use the fixtures.] Convey [story mood] through [specific geometry, palette and light relationship]. An unmistakable high-end animated environment at thumbnail size, with expressive form and polished CGI depth. No visible people or animal characters, silhouettes, crowds or character-shaped reflections. No religious imagery or identifiable third-party logos, trademarks or brand names.
```

## 失焦时的修正

| 出图偏差 | 优先调整 |
| --- | --- |
| 像真实楼盘或室内效果图 | 强化门窗、屋顶和空间层级的大形；降低密集石纹、摄影参数和镜面反射的权重 |
| 过暗、冷得发灰 | 提升中间调和有来源的天空／室内补光，保留彩色反弹；不靠全局提亮洗平层次 |
| 太儿童玩具化 | 保留可用的建筑厚度、承重、尺度和接触影，缩减动物符号装饰 |
| 太花、无焦点 | 压回一个主色家族，只让强调色落在入口、动线或剧情物件 |
| 氛围到位但功能不清 | 补明确机位、活动空地、门窗方向及关键物件的位置关系 |
| 同类物件难以指认 | 明确数量与分开的位置；给剧情需要的参照物以颜色／轮廓差异，跨图保持其身份 |
| 重复物件抢占画面 | 减少并分组前景物件，留通行与站位空地；远处简洁交代规模 |
| 空间过于稀疏、缺生活质感 | 补回功能家具、完整织物、门窗层次及一组有用途的陈列；主动作面与路径继续清楚，背景细节成组分层 |
| 舞台很大但讲台很小 | 调机位和取景，让实际功能物及动作区可读；降低空白屏幕和装饰的权重 |
