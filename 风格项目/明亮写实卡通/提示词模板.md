# 提示词结构模板

按当前模块和用途选段，不要求独立设计提交全剧或填齐所有槽位。以下方括号由用户需求、相关剧本事实或明确标注的假设替换。模板用于组织写法，不是可直接提交给出图模型的完整资产提示词。**英文提示词正文不写任何参照作品或工作室名称。**

## 公共风格锚句

```text
An original high-end 3D animated world with expressive sculpted silhouettes, clearly stylized proportions, broad coordinated color planes, softly painted material texture, luminous color bounce, and graphic yet spatially coherent shadows.
```

根据任务时间与天气把 `bright` 和 `lighting` 具体化；不强行把夜景改成日景。正文不要只放这一句，应从当前资产可见内容开始。

## A01｜场景

默认 16:9 空镜。使用一段完整英文正文，依次填入：

```text
A fixed 16:9 landscape frame with the composition designed for its full horizontal width. A clearly stylized, original cinematic 3D animated [interior/exterior] in [task-grounded location and moment], built around [one distinctive architectural silhouette] and [coordinated major color masses]. The camera is [position and viewing direction]. In the foreground [readable shape]; the midground holds [one visual anchor and a usable action area]; the background reveals [depth, access or exterior connection]. [Architecture and functional details] use expressive sculpted forms with believable construction. [If relevant, explain how the named species or body sizes use doors, seats, steps and fixtures.] Add [necessary task-relevant traces, if any]. [Main light source] enters from [direction] and falls on [surface], with [fill/bounce/practical light] and softened, color-designed shadows. Tie four to eight Hex colors to actual materials: [material #HEX, ...]. Use broad matte or satin surfaces, softened painted-like texture, selective highlights and clear dimensional contact. Empty environment with no visible people or animal characters, silhouettes, crowds or character-shaped reflections. No religious imagery or identifiable third-party logos, trademarks or brand names. An unmistakable animated feature frame, free of photorealistic real-estate-render aesthetics.
```

延展 A02 等要独立重述具体功能、材料、光色和与 A01 的一个可见连续性线索。Snn 使用获认可的基础成图做**图片编辑**，按原项目规则锁定原图，只改明确状态变量。

场景填槽时优先明确实际功能物及动作区，再补建筑气质。重复物件按剧情给准确数量、分开的可见位置和必要颜色／轮廓区别；删减无用装饰，光影强化层次。可爱的增量来自柔和轮廓、少量比例设计与柔化材质，程度服从场所情绪。可直接拼装的最新场景专用语句见[场景风格定调模块](场景风格定调模块.md)。

## C01｜兽脸与基础形体定角

先用角色简报确定物种、年龄感、头骨与口鼻、眼部表演、固定花纹、耳尾爪蹄、身体量感和神态。兽脸近景与完整基础形体可分开测试，第二步必须使用已认可的兽脸作为身份参考。基础形体采用非性化、自然皮毛覆盖的中性站姿，不刻画裸露解剖细节。近景和全身图的画幅按首次测试需要选择，**每条英文提示词正文自行写明画幅**。公共风格语法与神态槽位见 [角色定调/参考图学习与角色定调.md](角色定调/参考图学习与角色定调.md)。

初次原创定角不能直接把用户参考图中的具体狐狸、狼、雪豹或鹿角色作为新身份。角色获用户认可后，使用其成图锁定后续换装。

## C01-L01｜独立角色服装板

以下是**已有认可的兽脸与基础形体后**的占位结构，默认 16:9、全白 `#FFFFFF` 背景。此动画分支的新角色可像命名配角一样原创定脸，不要求先提供真人脸模。用户若明确指定角色身份参考，则改为锁定该参考。

```text
Use the uploaded approved animal face and base-form images as the identity reference for this original anthropomorphic [species] character in a bright cinematic 3D animated world. Present a fixed 16:9 landscape character sheet on a pure #FFFFFF background: a large high-resolution head view on the left, followed by full-body front, exact 90-degree side and back views on the right. Preserve the approved [head silhouette, eye and muzzle structure, markings, ears, tail, paws or hooves, body proportions and fixed identifier] across all views. The character is [script-grounded identity, setting, season, social level and scene function], wearing [one coherent outfit with silhouette, neckline, shoulder, waist, lower-body shape, material layers, colors, closures and footwear if appropriate]. [Explain ear, horn, tail, wing or paw accommodation only where relevant.] [Hair/fur grooming, makeup if relevant, and permitted jewelry] support [one readable design axis]. Fabric has thickness, gravity, contact shadows and natural deformation around the body. Keep the same facial identity, markings, anatomy, outfit and accessories in every view. Both hands or paws are empty. [Add applicable coverage, no-brand and no-religion constraints.]
```

初次定角时完整定义物种、年龄感、头部骨骼、眼鼻口、毛色花纹和固定识别点；定角图获认可后作为后续造型的身份参考。后续换装时继续明确引用已认可角色图，锁定物种、脸部结构和固定花纹，只更新造型。群演组采用 `G01` 等编号，仍按独立的组图规范。

## P01｜道具多视图板

默认 4:3 纯白背景：

```text
An original stylized 3D animated product design sheet for [one story-relevant prop], scaled for [the confirmed character/species and how it is handled]. On a pure white 4:3 background, show one large complete primary view, two complete complementary views and only [necessary structural or story-state close-ups]. Keep dimensions, shape, materials, colors, markings and wear identical in every view. Its [grip/hinge/closure/weight] is functional for [the user's anatomy and action]. Use [specific materials, surface finish, restrained color relationship], with soft studio lighting and accurate contact shadows. Show [exact script-required condition]. Only [verified essential text] is readable. No hands, characters, environment, religious imagery or identifiable third-party branding.
```

## 封面｜概念选定后

默认 3:4；需要探索时给适量不同概念，已选方案或直达要求直接写提示词。以下按概念删除不适用人物、关系与符号：

```text
An original 3:4 cinematic 3D animated cover with [one clear emotional promise]. Use the uploaded approved character references to preserve the exact species and facial identities of [characters]. [Main character] occupies [position and visual weight], with [head direction, eye target and restrained expression]. [Other characters, only if needed, form the chosen relationship with clear depth and size hierarchy.] [Composition and color relationships] lead the eye toward [the chosen focus, which may include the title]. Light comes from [specific sources], shaping [bright or story-appropriate color relationship] across characters and environment. Set the exact English title [EXACT TITLE] in [line breaks, size hierarchy, type roles, placement and safe margins], preserving the complete title, with key words readable at the intended phone thumbnail size. No other text, religious imagery or identifiable third-party branding.
```

每条正式提示词按需求及相关事实删去不适用占位和类别，保留一个完整英文文本块；不要把模板机械拼接成冗长清单。
