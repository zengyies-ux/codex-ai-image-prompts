Keep makeup fresh and natural. The top is fully opaque and lined where needed, with stable complete chest coverage in portrait, front and exact side view; no cleavage, low neckline, side-bust exposure, transparency, gaping or strained knit.

No text, no recognizable branding, no religious motifs, no bag, no handheld objects, no extra view, no duplicated body, no cropped shoes, no oversized full-body head, no short legs and no elongated torso.
